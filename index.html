Run the finish test before any full-speed driver

Your clearance risk is the follow-through. The finish test films slow full-finish swings with no ball so you can see exactly where the clubhead peaks against 104 in.

Never steer the finish to protect the ceiling

If driver only clears by consciously cutting the follow-through, keep it outdoors. Abbreviating means decelerating through impact, which costs more than the indoor reps gain.
